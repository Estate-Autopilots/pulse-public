# Pulse optional activity features

Attendance buttons, tasks, chats and leave work without activity sensing. Optional focus, idle and export metadata stays paused until you accept the published purpose notice and device permission in Pulse and confirm your work window.

Pulse excludes screenshots, typing, passwords, clipboard contents, window titles, private browsing and off-shift activity. Files, local paths and raw coordinates stay on the device. Missing signals never prove absence.

Pause stops capture; withdrawal clears unsent metadata. Revoke an app in Pulse Settings → Devices. Device credentials use the operating system credential store. Inspect permitted receipts and request corrections in Pulse.

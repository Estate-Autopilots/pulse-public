Pulse privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026. Applies to the Pulse app for Android and iPhone and the Pulse web app at pulse.estateautopilots.com.

Pulse is a work app. Your employer (the company that invited you) uses it to run its team's day: tasks, chats, attendance and requests. Pulse is made by Estate Autopilots, a brand of Vikriya Solutions LLP. Your employer decides what work information is kept in its Pulse; we run the service for them.

What Pulse collects

  • Your account: your name, work e-mail or username, and a protected password (or your company Google sign-in).
  • Your work: the tasks, messages, files, photos, voice notes and requests you and your colleagues add.
  • Attendance: when you check in and out, and the office or site you choose.
  • Location, only when you use it: when you check in or take a site photo, Pulse asks for your location once. If you turn on automatic office arrival, Android tells Pulse when you arrive at or leave your office's area (a geofence set by your company). Pulse does not track where you go and never reads your location in the background for anything else. You can turn this off in Pulse or in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Wi-Fi name: if your company uses office Wi-Fi for check-in, Pulse reads the name of the Wi-Fi you are connected to when you check in.
  • Camera and microphone: only when you take a photo, record a voice note or join a call in Pulse.
  • Device and app health: a device identifier for signing this phone in, a notification token, the app version, and timing and error reports that help us keep Pulse fast and working.

How it is used

Only to run Pulse for your company: to show your work, send your notifications, record attendance, and keep the service secure and working. We do not sell your information, show ads, or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

People in your company see what Pulse's roles allow them to see (for example, your manager and HR see your attendance). We use service providers to host and run Pulse; they handle data only for us. We share information with authorities only when the law requires it.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in credentials on the phone are kept in the phone's secure storage. Access inside Pulse is checked by role on every request.

How long it is kept

Work records are kept while your company uses Pulse and as its records policy requires. When you leave, your company can close your account; the records it must keep (such as attendance and payroll) stay with your company.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can sign out of the app at any time, which removes your Pulse sign-in from the phone, and uninstalling the app removes everything it stored there. To see, correct or delete your information, or to close your Pulse account, ask your company's Pulse admin or HR, or e-mail us at marketing@estateautopilots.com with the subject "Pulse data request". We reply within 30 days. Records your company is required to keep may be retained for that period.

Children

Pulse is for people at work and is not meant for children under 18.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: marketing@estateautopilots.com, Vikriya Solutions LLP (Estate Autopilots), estateautopilots.com.
